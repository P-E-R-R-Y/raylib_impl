Detailed Description

raylib music stream.

Two departures from raylib :

  • the three-state machine (stopped/playing/paused) is tracked here, raylib being unable to tell "paused" from "stopped" ;
  • setTime() clamps the position, SeekMusicStream() wrapping past the end.
